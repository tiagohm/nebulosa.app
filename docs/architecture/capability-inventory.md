# Capability Inventory

Part of the [architecture](../ARCHITECTURE.md) of Nebulosa.

Status: **Implemented** (works end to end), **Integration Required** (exists in `core`, not exposed by `api`/`web`), **Partial**, **Planned** (nothing yet).

- **Shell, routes, workspaces, status bar** (Implemented): `web/src/shell/` (`route.ts`, `workspaces.ts`, `WorkspaceLayout.tsx`, `statusbar/`)
- **About page (distribution identity)** (Implemented): `web/src/about/`, route `/about` opened from the bottom of the navigation rail, over `GET /api/about` (`api/src/about.ts`); commits and compile time inlined by `app/build.ts`, absent when running from source
- **Profiles (versioned)** (Implemented): `web/src/profile/` (`schema.ts` with `PROFILE_SETTINGS_VERSION`, store and actions in `profile.ts`, synchronization with the server in `sync.ts`), kept by `api/src/workspace.ts`; the active profile and the view fields the server never keeps are the tab's, in browser storage (`view.ts`)
- **Connections (INDI, Alpaca, simulator)** (Implemented): `api/src/connection.ts`, `web/src/connection/`
- **Equipment Setup (roles, telescope and guide telescope optics)** (Implemented): `web/src/equipment/` (`roles.ts`, `equipment.ts`, `EquipmentModal.tsx`, `availability.ts`), opened from Management
- **Server file browser** (Implemented): `api/src/files.ts`, `web/src/files/` (`FileExplorerModal`: Open Image, Save to Server, and the directory and file choices of the camera operations, Calibration Library, and Image Stacking)
- **Device registry and identity** (Implemented): `api/src/devices.ts` (`DeviceSummary`: connection id + stable device id)
- **Device capabilities and control states** (Implemented): `api/src/devices.ts` (`DeviceStateMap`, `<type>:updated` and `camera:capabilities` events), `GET /api/connections/:id/states` of `connection.ts`; mirrored by `controlRuntime` (`web/src/equipment-control/control.ts`)
- **INDI properties (raw vectors of every device)** (Implemented): `api/src/indi.ts` over the shared `IndiPropertyRegistry` of `DeviceRegistry.properties` (directory, subscribed stream, arbitrated writes); `<message>` elements in `api/src/indimessages.ts` (global ones as notifications, device ones in memory for the Messages view); Equipment Control views `web/src/equipment-control/indi/`
- **Operations and arbitration** (Implemented): `api/src/operations.ts` (`OperationRegistry`), history of past operations `api/src/operationhistory.ts` (`operation_runs`), `web/src/operations.ts`; toolkit `core/src/devices/orchestration/`
- **Mount commands (slew, goto, flip, sync, stop, park, home, tracking, slew rate, site, time)** (Implemented): `api/src/mounts.ts`; Sky slew and stop `web/src/sky/mount.ts`; held nudges of `api/src/leases.ts`, manual moves through the Images Mount Nudge; guide outputs (pulse, guide rate) in `api/src/guideoutputs.ts`
- **Mount pointing model** (Implemented): `api/src/mountpointing.ts` (library) with `.sessions.ts`, `.frames.ts`, `.fit.ts`, `.worker.ts`, `.corrections.ts`, `.store.ts` over `core/src/observation/mount/pointing.ts`; `web/src/mountpointing/`; Images Pointing Model Builder `web/src/camera/PointingModelBuilderPanel.tsx`, Tools Mount Pointing Model `web/src/tools/mountpointing/`
- **Focuser, wheel, rotator, cover, flat panel, dew heater, dome, camera cooling** (Implemented): `api/src/{focusers,wheels,rotators,covers,flatpanels,dewheaters,domes,cameras}.ts`, Equipment Control page
- **Focuser backlash compensation** (Implemented): `api/src/focuserbacklash.ts` (store, `focuser_backlash` of the operational database), applied by `FocuserCommands`; `web/src/equipment-control/controls/FocuserBacklash.tsx`
- **Equipment Control (manual device control)** (Implemented): `web/src/equipment-control/`, route `/equipment` opened from Management
- **Camera capture and frames** (Implemented): `api/src/captures.ts` over `CameraCapturer`; Images Camera Acquisition tool `web/src/images/CameraAcquisitionTool.tsx` over `CameraAcquisitionSections` (`web/src/camera/CameraCapturePanel.tsx`), `capture.ts`
- **Live stacking** (Implemented): `api/src/livestacking.ts` over the capture frame sink and the toolkit `LiveStacker` (`livestacking.engine.ts`, `livestacking.groups.ts`, on the worker `livestacking.worker.ts` of each session); saved stacks in `live_stack_products` (`livestacking.store.ts`); Images Live Stacking tool `web/src/images/LiveStackingTool.tsx` over `LiveStackingSections`
- **Camera tabs in Images (Latest, saved frames carousel, held frame)** (Implemented): `api/src/captureframes.ts`; `web/src/images/captureframes.ts`, `cameratabs.ts`, `images.ts`, `ImagesWorkspace.tsx`, `CaptureFramesCarousel.tsx`
- **Image documents (FITS/XISF)** (Implemented): `api/src/images.ts` + `images.worker.ts`, `web/src/images/images.ts`
- **Display pipeline, save and download (FITS, XISF, JPEG)** (Implemented): same as above; viewer toolbar `web/src/images/DisplayToolbar.tsx`, `SaveImagePopover.tsx`, `SaveImageModal.tsx`
- **Histogram and statistics** (Planned): a planned section of the Image Inspector (`PlannedControls`)
- **External tools settings (ASTAP, local astrometry.net, nova.astrometry.net, MediaMTX)** (Implemented): `api/src/externaltools.ts`, `web/src/settings/`, route `/settings/external-tools` opened from the top bar gear
- **Observing sites (named places shared by every profile, one active per profile)** (Implemented): `api/src/workspace.ts`, `web/src/location/`, `web/src/settings/ObservingSitesSettings.tsx`, the top bar site menu, route `/settings/observing-sites`
- **Plate solving** (Implemented): `api/src/platesolving.ts`; Images tool `web/src/images/PlateSolverPanel.tsx`
- **Star detection** (Implemented): `api/src/stars.ts` + `stars.worker.ts`, `web/src/stars/`; Images tool, Auto Focus, Guiding (`api/src/guideregions.ts`)
- **Star Profile Inspector** (Implemented): `api/src/starprofile.ts` + `starprofile.analysis.ts`, `starprofile.worker.ts`, `web/src/stars/starprofile.ts`; Images tool `web/src/images/StarProfileInspectorPanel.tsx`
- **Streak detection** (Implemented): `api/src/streaks.ts` + `streaks.worker.ts` over `core/src/imaging/analysis/streak/`, `web/src/streaks/`; Images tool `web/src/images/StreakDetectionPanel.tsx`
- **Image annotation** (Implemented): `api/src/annotations.ts` over the field queries of `api/src/sky/catalog.ts`, solar-system objects through SkyBoT (`core/src/adapters/orbits/skybot.ts`), `web/src/annotations/`; Images tool `web/src/images/AnnotationPanel.tsx`
- **WCS** (Implemented): TAN/TAN-SIP through `core/src/astrometry/wcs/fits.wcs.ts`; viewer geometry `web/src/platesolving/wcs.ts`
- **Shared image viewer** (Implemented): `web/src/viewer/` (overlays in image pixels, zoom/pan in `transform.ts`)
- **Overlays** (Partial): Reticle (`web/src/viewer/Reticle.tsx`), star markers (`web/src/stars/StarOverlay.tsx`), the star of the Star Profile Inspector (`web/src/stars/StarProfileOverlay.tsx`), streak lines (`web/src/streaks/StreakOverlay.tsx`), annotation (`web/src/annotations/AnnotationOverlay.tsx`), WCS (`web/src/platesolving/WcsOverlay.tsx`), RA/Dec grid (`WcsGridOverlay.tsx`), crosshair, orientation and scale bar (`WcsAidsOverlay.tsx`), measurements and markers (`web/src/images/MeasurementOverlay.tsx`), Target Offset, also of Target Centering (`web/src/images/TargetOffsetOverlay.tsx`, `web/src/camera/targetcentering.overlay.ts`), TPPA correction guide (`web/src/camera/TppaOverlay.tsx`), DARV trails (`web/src/camera/DarvOverlay.tsx`), guider (`web/src/camera/GuidingOverlay.tsx`); no ROI overlay
- **Planetarium, search, inspector, time, observer** (Implemented): `web/src/sky/` (canvas renderer in `celestial/`), `api/src/sky/` over the generated `api/data/sky.catalog.db` (`bin/src/sky.catalog.ts`)
- **Sun Inspector (almanac, SDO image, NOAA active regions, next solar eclipse)** (Implemented): `web/src/sky/sun/` over `api/src/sky/sun.ts`, `sdo.ts`, `activeregions.ts`, and `/api/eclipses/solar`; see [Sun Inspector Contract](sun-inspector.md)
- **Moon Inspector (almanac, drawing, phases, apsides, next lunar eclipse)** (Implemented): `web/src/sky/moon/` over `api/src/sky/moon.ts`, the generated `web/assets/moon/`, and `/api/eclipses/lunar`; see [Moon Inspector Contract](moon-inspector.md)
- **Image processing (derived images beyond the display pipeline)** (Implemented): `api/src/imageprocessing.ts` + `imageprocessing.worker.ts`; Images tool `web/src/images/ImageProcessingPanel.tsx`, store `imageprocessing.ts`, catalog `imageprocessing.catalog.ts`
- **FOV and optical presets** (Implemented): `web/src/sky/framing.ts` (gnomonic outline, position angle), Sky Framing panel `FramingPanel.tsx`; camera sensors of the Equipment Setup `web/src/sky/sensors.ts`
- **HiPS surveys** (Integration Required): `core/src/adapters/sky/hips2fits.ts`, `core/src/astronomy/sky/spatial/healpix.ts`
- **Lunar eclipses and map** (Implemented): Lunar Eclipse Map tool (`web/src/tools/lunareclipse/`) over `api/src/lunareclipses.ts` and the conventions of `api/src/eclipses.ts`, drawn on `WorldMap` (`web/src/ui/WorldMap.tsx`)
- **Solar eclipses and map** (Implemented): Solar Eclipse Map tool (`web/src/tools/solareclipse/`) over `api/src/solareclipses.ts`, `solareclipses.map.ts` on `solareclipses.worker.ts`, drawn on `WorldMap`
- **Calculator formulas** (Implemented): Calculator tool (`web/src/tools/calculator/`) over `core/src/astronomy/formulas.ts` and Sky Framing's `resolveFraming`; computed in the browser
- **Close approaches of small bodies** (Implemented): Close Approaches tool (`web/src/tools/closeapproaches/`) over `api/src/closeapproaches.ts`, JPL CAD and SBDB through the queue of `api/src/ssd.ts`; orbit rendering and MPC observations are not provided
- **Astro Weather (forecast and night planning)** (Implemented): Astro Weather tool (`web/src/tools/astroweather/`) over `api/src/astroweather.ts`, Open-Meteo (`astroweather.openmeteo.ts`) and 7Timer! (`astroweather.seventimer.ts`), with the local night of `astroweather.night.ts`; forecasts only, never local sensors or automation
- **Observing Planner (target discovery, night analysis, manual night plans)** (Implemented): `api/src/observingplanner.ts` (service and routes), `observingplanner.astronomy.ts` (nights, windows, scores) over `core/src/astronomy/events/visibility.ts` and `core/src/astronomy/planning.ts`, `observingplanner.minorbodies.ts` (trajectories of asteroids and comets) with `observingplanner.minorbodies.store.ts`, `observingplanner.schedule.ts`, `observingplanner.store.ts`, and the catalog listing `queryCatalog` of `api/src/sky/catalog.ts`; the tool `web/src/tools/observingplanner/`
- **Jupiter Observing Planner (Galilean phenomena, Great Red Spot transits, site visibility)** (Implemented): Jupiter Observing Planner tool (`web/src/tools/jupiter/`) over `api/src/jupiter.ts` (service and routes), `jupiter.astronomy.ts`, and `jupiter.store.ts`, over the Jovian ephemeris source, geometry, phenomena, and mutual-event finder of `core/src/astronomy/ephemeris/jovian.ts` and `core/src/astronomy/events/jupiter*.ts`/`mutual.ts`; geocentric contacts only, no light curves, and the Great Red Spot longitude is entered by the operator
- **Saturn Observing Planner (ring seasons, major moons, phenomena, site visibility)** (Implemented): Saturn Observing Planner tool (`web/src/tools/saturn/`) over `api/src/saturn.ts` (service and routes), `saturn.astronomy.ts`, and `saturn.events.ts` over the shared `planetary.source.ts`, and the Saturnian ephemeris, ring geometry, and satellite geometry of `core/src/astronomy/ephemeris/saturnian.ts`, `core/src/astronomy/events/saturn.rings.ts`, `saturn.geometry.ts`, `saturn.phenomena.ts`, and the source-injected Saturnian mutual-event finder; ring occultations, ring shadows, and photometry are not provided
- **Artificial satellites (catalog, passes, ephemerides, ground tracks)** (Partial): `api/src/satellites.ts` over `core/src/astronomy/events/satellite.ts`, the CelesTrak downloads of `satellites.sync.ts`, and the catalog of `satellites.store.ts`; Artificial Satellites tool `web/src/tools/artificialsatellites/` (Ground Track on `WorldMap`); Sky search, Satellite Inspector (`web/src/sky/satellite/`), and map markers (`web/src/sky/satellites.sky.ts`); mount tracking, brightness, and screening are not provided
- **SPICE Kernel Library (SPK files for a local accurate ephemeris)** (Implemented): `api/src/spicekernels.ts` (curated NAIF downloads, Horizons small-body generations through the queue of `api/src/ssd.ts`, uploads), `spicekernels.store.ts`, and the runtime set of `spicekernels.runtime.ts` over the multi-file resolver of `core/src/astronomy/ephemeris/kernels/spk.kernelset.ts`; SPICE Kernel Library tool `web/src/tools/spicekernels/`; only J2000 SPK segments of types 2, 3, 9, and 21 are evaluated, and frame, orientation, clock, and mission kernels are not provided
- **Astronomical time conversion** (Implemented): Astronomical Time Converter tool (`web/src/tools/astronomicaltime/`) and the status bar clock (`web/src/shell/statusbar/astronomicalclock.ts`) over `api/src/astronomicaltime.ts` and the Earth orientation tables of `api/src/astronomicaltime.eop.ts`, which the whole server reads; BJD, HJD, and FITS exposure timing are not provided
- **Mount remote control (LX200, Stellarium)** (Implemented): Mount Remote Control tool (`web/src/tools/mountremotecontrol/`) over `api/src/mountremotecontrol.ts` and the protocol servers of `core/src/devices/protocols/`
- **Observatory cameras (RTSP security cameras relayed over WebRTC)** (Implemented): Observatory Cameras tool (`web/src/tools/observatorycameras/`, mini-player `CameraViewerHost` in the shell) over `api/src/observatorycameras.ts` and its store, secret, gateway, and WHEP modules; the MediaMTX gateway is an external tool installed by the operator
- **Alpaca server** (Integration Required): `core/src/devices/alpaca/server.ts`, `discovery.ts`; the Tools entry `alpaca-server` is planned (no view)
- **Native guiding** (Implemented): `api/src/guiding.ts` + `guiding.worker.ts`, `guideregions.ts`, `guidingprofile.ts` over the interactive guiding session of `core/src/observation/guiding/`, mirrored by `web/src/camera/guiding.ts`; Images Guiding tool `web/src/images/GuidingTool.tsx`
- **Auto focus** (Implemented): `api/src/autofocus.ts` + `autofocus.curve.ts` over `runAutoFocus`; Images Auto Focus tool `web/src/images/AutoFocusTool.tsx` over `web/src/camera/AutoFocusPanel.tsx`, `FocusCurve.tsx`, store `autofocus.ts`
- **TPPA** (Implemented): `api/src/tppa.ts` over `acquireThreePointPolarAlignment` and `refineThreePointPolarAlignment` (`core/src/observation/alignment/tppa.runner.ts`); Images TPPA tool `web/src/images/TppaTool.tsx`
- **DARV** (Implemented): `api/src/darv.ts` over `runDarv` (`core/src/observation/alignment/darv.runner.ts`), `api/src/darv.analysis.ts` over `analyzeDarvImage` on `darv.worker.ts`; Images DARV tool `web/src/images/DarvTool.tsx`
- **Flat analysis and exposure** (Implemented): `core/src/imaging/analysis/flat/`; `api/src/flatwizard.ts` session over `api/src/flatwizard.analysis.ts`, which measures acquired FITS files in DN on a worker; Images → Flat Wizard
- **Dark acquisition** (Implemented): `api/src/darklibrarywizard.ts` session over `api/src/darklibrarywizard.analysis.ts` and `api/src/darklibrarywizard.plan.ts` (worker `darklibrarywizard.worker.ts`): capture plans of DARK frames and dark-flats, kept in memory, with light protection and thermal stabilization, handed to the Calibration Library; Images → Dark Library Wizard
- **Calibration library** (Implemented): `core/src/imaging/processing/calibration.integration.ts`; `api/src/calibrationlibrary.ts` with `.compatibility.ts`, `.sources.ts`, `.store.ts`, `.jobs.ts`, `.analysis.ts`, `.worker.ts`; Calibration Library tool `web/src/tools/calibrationlibrary/`, store `web/src/calibrationlibrary/`
- **Image stacking** (Implemented): Projects, imports, filter groups, calibration pins, analysis and registration, integration into linear masters, color composition: `api/src/imagestacking.ts` with `.sources.ts`, `.store.ts`, `.analysis.ts`, `.integration.ts`, `.composition.ts`, `.worker.ts`; `web/src/tools/imagestacking/`; toolkit primitives in `core/src/imaging/processing/stacking.combination.ts` and `channel.composition.ts`
- **Target Centering** (Implemented): `api/src/targetcentering.ts` over `centerTarget` (`core/src/observation/mount/centering.ts`); Images Target Centering tool `web/src/images/TargetCenteringTool.tsx` over `web/src/camera/TargetCenteringPanel.tsx`, store `web/src/camera/targetcentering.ts`
- **Sequencer** (Planned): No engine exists in `core` or `api`
- **Notifications** (Implemented): `api/src/notifications.ts` (history, persistence, `GET /api/notifications`, `notification:created`), `api/src/notifications.rules.ts` (classifier), `web/src/notifications/` (store, bell, panel, toasts, targets)
- **Meridian flip and mount limits** (Partial): `core/src/observation/mount/meridian.flip.ts` classifies the window of the read-only Meridian Flip Monitor (`api/src/meridianflip.ts`), and the Meridian Flip Executor (`api/src/meridianflipexecutor.ts`) flips by native flip or forced GoTo with verified, inferred, or unverified evidence; `limits.ts` is not integrated
- **Mosaic planning and night scheduling** (Integration Required): `core/src/observation/framing/mosaic.plan.ts`, `core/src/observation/scheduler.ts`
- **PHD2 guiding** (Integration Required): `core/src/observation/guiding/phd2/`
- **Power, weather, safety monitor, GPS devices** (Integration Required): Toolkit managers for power, weather, and safety monitor (`core/src/devices/indi/manager/`) and commanders for power and weather; GPS is only a device type; not managed by `api`; their INDI vectors are in INDI Properties

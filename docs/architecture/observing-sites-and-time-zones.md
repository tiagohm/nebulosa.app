# Observing Sites and Time Zones Contract

Part of the [architecture](../ARCHITECTURE.md) of Nebulosa.

| Concept                         | Owner                                                           | Meaning                                                                                                                                          |
| ------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Observing site                  | `api`, database (`observing_sites`), mirrored in `siteState`    | A named place: geodetic latitude and longitude (degrees, east positive), height above the WGS84 ellipsoid (meters), and its IANA civil time zone |
| Active site                     | `Profile.activeSiteId` (`profiles.active_site_id`)              | The site a profile observes from; none is a valid state, and then nothing assumes a place                                                        |
| Display zone                    | `ProfileSettings.dateTime` (Settings → Date & Time)             | How civil times read in every screen of the profile: the site's zone, UTC, or the browser's                                                      |
| Tool location and zone override | tab memory of each tool (`ToolLocation`, `DisplayZoneOverride`) | A place and a zone chosen for one tool alone: another saved site or coordinates typed in with their zone; never written to the profile or a site |
| Instants                        | every request and result                                        | Unix milliseconds (UTC), or the toolkit's scales; never changed by a zone                                                                        |
| Mount site                      | the mount driver                                                | The site the mount reports; it equals a site only once Send Active Site to Mount wrote it                                                        |

- The catalog is one per server, shared by every profile, tab, and browser, and edited in Settings → Observing Sites (`web/src/settings/ObservingSitesSettings.tsx`, `ObservingSiteEditor.tsx`): a name, coordinates typed as decimal or sexagesimal degrees with a sign or a hemisphere letter (`parseGeographicAngle`) and kept in decimal degrees, the ellipsoidal height, and a zone always chosen by a person (the browser's is only offered); the world map sets the coordinates and never the zone. Names and coordinates may repeat; ids are generated by the tab. The top bar's site menu selects the active site in one click. Selecting, editing, or deleting a site changes the workspace only: no device is commanded, no operation restarted, and a running operation keeps the place it started with (operations receive coordinates by value, or read the mount's own site).
- `web/src/location/` derives the rest, storing nothing: `activeSite`, `resolveToolLocation` (the active site followed live, a saved site, or typed coordinates whose zone is UTC until chosen), `siteObserver` (the `{ latitude, longitude, elevation }` the API takes), and `locationKey` (geographic only, so a renamed site or a new zone reloads nothing that depends on the place alone). `ToolLocationSection` is the Location section every tool with a place of its own uses, and `TimeZoneSelect` the zone override every tool that offers one uses.
- One rule shows a civil time (`resolveDisplayZone` of `web/src/location/zone.ts`): a tool's override when it made one (an IANA zone, UTC, the browser's, or the site of the tool), otherwise the profile's display zone; the site mode takes the tool's own location (a saved site other than the active one, or typed coordinates), and without one the browser's zone stands in, named as such. Zones are always IANA identifiers, never offsets or an empty sentinel; offsets are shown for an instant only (`formatUtcOffset`).
- A local day or night (`localDay` of the Sun almanac, `/api/astro-weather/night`) is always named in the zone of the place observed, whatever zone the times are shown in. LMST, LAST, hour angles, rise, transit, set, passes, and visibility depend on the place and the instant only; UTC, Julian dates, and timescales keep their own definitions.
- Wall times typed in a zone (`wallTimeToInstant`) name one instant, two when the clocks repeat them, or none when they skip them: the Sky time bar shows the first of two and refuses a skipped one, each saying so; the Astronomical Time Converter keeps its Earlier and Later choice and refuses a skipped reading through its server.

# Routes and Events

Part of the [architecture](../ARCHITECTURE.md) of Nebulosa.

Route prefixes, each owned by the module named:

- `/api/health`, `/api/ws`: `api/src/index.ts`
- `/api/about`: `about.ts`, composed by `app/src/main.ts` (version, build commits and time, server runtime and platform)
- `/api/connections` (including `/:id/devices`, `/:id/states`): `connection.ts`
- `/api/connections/:id/indi`: `indi.ts` (directory of the physical INDI devices of a connection, and the writes of their vectors)
- `/api/connections/:id/<device type>/:deviceId[/<command>]`: one commands module per device type: `mounts`, `guide-outputs`, `focusers`, `wheels`, `rotators`, `covers`, `flat-panels`, `dew-heaters`, `domes`, `cameras` (`mounts.ts` … `cameras.ts`); a camera's `/capture` (with `/stop`, `/abort`), and the `/autofocus`, `/tppa`, `/mount-pointing`, `/target-centering`, `/darv`, `/flat-wizard`, `/dark-library-wizard`, `/live-stacking`, and `/guiding` starts belong to `captures.ts`, `autofocus.ts`, `tppa.ts`, `mountpointing.sessions.ts`, `targetcentering.ts`, `darv.ts`, `flatwizard.ts`, `darklibrarywizard.ts`, `livestacking.ts`, and `guiding.ts`; a mount's `/remote-control/:protocol` belongs to `mountremotecontrol.ts`
- `/api/notifications`: `notifications.ts` (the retained notification history, newest first)
- `/api/operations`: `operations.ts`
- `/api/operations/history`: `operationhistory.ts` (the start and end of past operations, newest first, paged)
- `/api/mount-remote-control`, `/api/connections/:id/mounts/:deviceId/remote-control/:protocol`: `mountremotecontrol.ts` (snapshot of the LX200 and Stellarium listeners; `/start`, `/stop`, and `DELETE /clients/:clientId`)
- `/api/meridian-flip`, `/api/connections/:id/mounts/:deviceId/meridian-flip`: `meridianflip.ts` (snapshot of the Meridian Flip Monitors; `/enable`, `/disable`, `PATCH /settings`, `GET /transitions`, and the read-only `/assess-exposure`)
- `/api/meridian-flip/executions`, `/api/connections/:id/mounts/:deviceId/meridian-flip/{execute,arm,disarm,eligibility}`: `meridianflipexecutor.ts` (snapshot and executions of the Meridian Flip Executor; `/executions/:sessionId/cancel`)
- `/api/mounts/nudges`, `/api/domes/motions`: `mounts.ts` and `domes.ts` (the running held motions, `/:token/heartbeat`, `/:token/stop`), over the `LeasedMotions` of `leases.ts`
- `/api/captures`, `/api/autofocus`, `/api/tppa`, `/api/target-centering`, `/api/darv`, `/api/flat-wizard`, `/api/dark-library-wizard`, `/api/guiding`: snapshots and sessions of `captures.ts`, `autofocus.ts`, `tppa.ts`, `targetcentering.ts`, `darv.ts`, `flatwizard.ts`, `darklibrarywizard.ts`, `guiding.ts`
- `/api/calibration-library`: `calibrationlibrary.ts` (snapshot, refresh, frames with their exclusions and overrides, imports, groups, masters, recipe validation, deletions, metadata-only match preview)
- `/api/image-stacking`: `imagestacking.ts` (snapshot, projects, imports, frames, filter groups, file checks, cohorts and their calibration, settings, analyses, validation, preflight, runs, products, compositions, composites)
- `/api/live-stacking`: `livestacking.ts` (snapshot, sessions by id: pause, resume, stop, abort, reset, new field, calibration, snapshot views, exports; saved stacks)
- `/api/mount-pointing/sessions`, `/api/mount-pointing/plan`: `mountpointing.sessions.ts` (acquisition sessions, `/pause`, `/resume`, `/stop`, the preview of a plan, which commands nothing, and `/models/:modelId/verify`)
- `/api/mount-pointing/activations`, `/api/mount-pointing/models/:modelId/activate`, `/api/mount-pointing/mounts/:connectionId/:deviceId/deactivate`: `mountpointing.corrections.ts` (the models in force)
- `/api/mount-pointing/models`: `mountpointing.ts` (the model library: list, import, rename, delete, export, and fits)
- `/api/capture-frames`: `captureframes.ts`
- `/api/images`: `images.ts` (open, render, save and download of a copy as FITS, XISF, or JPEG); the analyses of a document belong to their services: `/:id/stars` to `stars.ts`, `/:id/star-profile` to `starprofile.ts`, `/:id/streaks` to `streaks.ts`, `/:id/plate-solving` and `/:id/plate-solutions` to `platesolving.ts`, `/:id/annotations` to `annotations.ts`, `/:id/processing` to `imageprocessing.ts`
- `/api/stars`, `/api/streaks`, `/api/plate-solving`, `/api/annotations`, `/api/image-processing`: the jobs of `stars.ts`, `streaks.ts`, `platesolving.ts`, `annotations.ts`, `imageprocessing.ts`
- `/api/settings/external-tools`: `externaltools.ts`
- `/api/observatory-cameras`: `observatorycameras.ts` (snapshot, `PUT` and `DELETE /:id` of the camera definitions, and the WHEP signaling of a browser: `POST /:id/whep`, `PATCH` and `DELETE /:id/whep/sessions/:token`)
- `/api/workspace`, `/api/saved-connections`, `/api/observing-sites`, `/api/profiles`: `workspace.ts` (the saved connections, the observing sites, and the profiles: their snapshot, and the writes of each unit, the notifications each profile read or dismissed included)
- `/api/files`: `files.ts` (server filesystem browser of the file chooser)
- `/api/sky`: `api/src/sky/` (`routes.ts`: catalog, search, objects, positions, bodies, minor bodies, constellations; `/api/sky/sun/almanac` of `sun.ts`, `/api/sky/sun/image` of `sdo.ts`, `/api/sky/sun/active-regions` of `activeregions.ts`, `/api/sky/moon/almanac` of `moon.ts`)
- `/api/eclipses/lunar`: `lunareclipses.ts` (eclipse navigation, global map, local circumstances)
- `/api/eclipses/solar`: `solareclipses.ts` (eclipse navigation, global map on a worker, local circumstances)
- `/api/astronomical-time`: `astronomicaltime.ts` (`/convert` of the Astronomical Time Converter, `/reference` of the status bar clock)
- `/api/astro-weather`: `astroweather.ts` (`/forecast` of the Open-Meteo and 7Timer! forecasts of a place, `/night` of the local Sun and Moon of a night)
- `/api/observing-planner`: `observingplanner.ts` (`/presets` of the constraint defaults, `/catalog` of the paged catalog listing, `/analyze` of one target over a night, `/discover` of a bounded batch of candidates, `/plans` with `GET`, `PUT`, and `DELETE /:id` and `/:id/duplicate` of the saved night plans, `/preview` of the schedule of a plan document, and `/minor-bodies/:spkId/refresh` of the saved orbital elements of a minor body)
- `/api/jupiter`: `jupiter.ts` (`/grs` `GET` and `PUT` of the server's Great Red Spot model, `/state` of the view at an instant, `/track` of the sampled satellite tracks over a range, `/events` of the phenomena over a range with their visibility from a site, `/source` of the readiness of the SPICE kernels)
- `/api/saturn`: `saturn.ts` (`/rings/state` of the disk and ring geometry at an instant, `/rings/timeline` of the ring openings and ring-plane events over a range, `/moons/state` of the rings and seven moons at an instant, `/moons/track` of the sampled moon tracks, `/moons/elongations` of the greatest separations, `/moons/events` of the phenomena with their visibility from a site, `/source` of the kernel readiness of each scope)
- `/api/satellites`: `satellites.ts` (catalog search, `/sources`, `/sources/:sourceKey/refresh`, and `/sources/:sourceKey/auto-download` of the CelesTrak downloads, `/import`, `/tracks` of the Sky, and `/:noradId` with `/element-source`, `/position`, `/passes`, `/ephemeris`, `/ground-track`) over `satellites.store.ts`, `satellites.sync.ts`, `satellites.auto.ts`, `satellites.celestrak.ts`, `satellites.elements.ts`, `satellites.ephemeris.ts`
- `/api/close-approaches`: `closeapproaches.ts` (pages of the JPL close-approach data, `/objects` of the identity and details of a body, `/objects/:spkId/approaches` of its encounters)
- `/api/spice-kernels`: `spicekernels.ts` (snapshot, `/catalog` of the curated downloads, `/coverage` of a NAIF body at an instant, `PATCH /settings` of the ephemeris policy, `/import` of an uploaded file, `/download` and `/generate` jobs, `/jobs/:jobId/cancel`, and `/:id` with `PATCH`, `DELETE`, and `/verify`)

Events of `/api/ws` (`ServerEvent`), by `type`: `connection:open`, `connection:close` (its `cause` is `requested` for a disconnect asked by a tab or the disposal of the registry, `lost` when the client itself reported the close), `device:added`, `device:updated`, `device:removed`, `<type>:updated` for the control state of each managed device type but the camera, whose state is `camera:capabilities`, `operation:updated`, `capture:updated`, `capture:frame`, `captureFrame:updated`, `captureSource:updated`, `autofocus:updated`, `tppa:updated`, `mountPointing:updated`, `mountPointing:activation`, `mountPointing:modelUpdated`, `mountPointing:modelRemoved`, `targetCentering:updated`, `darv:updated`, `flatWizard:updated`, `darkLibraryWizard:updated`, `calibrationLibrary:changed`, `calibrationLibrary:jobUpdated`, `calibrationLibrary:masterUpdated`, `imageStacking:changed`, `imageStacking:jobUpdated`, `liveStacking:updated`, `liveStacking:closed`, `liveStacking:products`, `guiding:updated`, `guiding:event`, `guiding:telemetry`, `guiding:frame`, `mount:nudge`, `mountRemoteControl:updated`, `meridianFlip:updated`, `meridianFlip:transition`, `dome:motion`, `focuserBacklash:updated`, `externalTool:updated`, `observatoryCamera:updated`, `observatoryCamera:removed`, `observatoryCamera:streamUpdated`, `observatoryCamera:gatewayUpdated` (broadcast directly; metadata only, never an address), `savedConnection:updated`, `savedConnection:removed`, `observingSite:updated`, `observingSite:removed`, `profile:updated`, `profile:removed`, `stars:updated`, `streaks:updated`, `plateSolving:updated`, `annotations:updated`, `imageProcessing:updated`, `notification:created`, `satellites:changed`, `spiceKernels:changed`, `spiceKernels:jobUpdated`, `observingPlan:updated` (the whole saved plan) and `observingPlan:removed` of the night plans of the Observing Planner, `jupiter:grsUpdated` (the saved Great Red Spot model with its revision) of the Jupiter Observing Planner, `indi:deviceAdded`, `indi:deviceUpdated`, `indi:deviceRemoved` for the INDI device directory, and `indi:globalMessage` and `indi:globalMessagesSuppressed` for the global messages of the INDI servers. Sent only to the socket that subscribed: `indi:snapshot`, `indi:propertyDefined`, `indi:propertyUpdated`, `indi:propertyRemoved`, and `indi:subscriptionError` of a property stream, and `indi:messagesSnapshot`, `indi:messageReceived`, and `indi:messagesSubscriptionError` of a message stream. Events never carry pixels or secrets (a frame is named by its image id); a client fetches pixels and full records through the route of their owner.

The events of the connections and devices, the operations, the pointing models and their activations, the Meridian Flip Monitors, the global messages of the INDI servers, and the sessions of Camera Acquisition, the camera operations, Calibration Library, Image Stacking, and Live Stacking are published through one `ServerEventDispatcher` (`api/src/events.ts`, `publish` of `api/src/index.ts`); the others (the workspace, the notifications, the held motions, Mount Remote Control, the focuser backlash, the external tools, the analysis jobs, the capture frames, the satellites, the SPICE Kernel Library, the night plans of the Observing Planner, the INDI directory) go to `broadcast` directly, so neither history nor an in-process observer sees them. The dispatcher's subscribers are, in order, the WebSocket broadcast, the operation history, and the notification history, so a tab sees a change before the notification about it, and a service of the process that observes the others (a caller composing tools, the Meridian Flip Monitors) subscribes after them and unsubscribes with the function `subscribe` returned. Delivery is synchronous, in memory only, and never replayed, so an event is an observation, never the outcome of a command; an observer that throws is reported and skipped, never reaching the next observers nor the device operation that published the event. Events another feature will need (a step of a sequencer run) are added to `ServerEvent` by the service that owns them, when it exists, and published through the same dispatcher, as the Meridian Flip Monitor publishes `meridianFlip:transition`.

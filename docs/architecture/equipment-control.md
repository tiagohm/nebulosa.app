# Equipment Control Contract

Part of the [architecture](../ARCHITECTURE.md) of Nebulosa.

- The page lists the devices of the active profile's connection only, grouped by hardware id, so the interfaces of one physical device (a flip-flat's cover and flat panel, a mount and its guide output) appear once. The INDI directory adds every device the connection reports that no interface manages, named in the URL by its hardware id (`web/src/equipment-control/groups.ts`). Each device also has read-only Diagnostics (`DeviceDiagnostics.tsx`, collapsed): the identity and driver of each interface, the capabilities it declares, its last errors, the operations holding it, and its raw state. Opening it, switching devices, or reloading never connects, disconnects, or commands anything.
- Every command goes through `api` under the shared contract of `api/src/commands.ts`: settings answer 204 once the driver reports the commanded state (504 `timeout` when it never does), motions answer 202 with an operation id and report their outcome only through `operation:updated`. Durable requests carry a client token, so a repeated request never moves a device twice; the web generates tokens and ids with `nanoid`, never `crypto.randomUUID`, which browsers only provide in a secure context (HTTPS or localhost) and the app is usually opened over plain HTTP on the local network.
- Stops never wait for a lease: `POST …/stop` cancels the operation of the physical device and sends the commander's `stopMotion` (`stopResponse` of `api/src/commands.ts`). A filter wheel has no abort. Guide pulse stops never cancel another operation of the mount.
- Manual motion held by a lease (mount nudge, dome rotation) shares `api/src/leases.ts` and `web/src/press.ts`.
- The state of a camera also says whether it is exposing (`exposing`), for whichever tool asked for the frame; the progress of an exposure stays with the capture that runs it, so this field changes only when an exposure starts or ends.
- The sensor temperature of a camera (`CCD_TEMPERATURE`) or a focuser (`FOCUS_TEMPERATURE`) is read by the toolkit's `ThermometerManager`, registered in `DeviceRegistry` after the managed types, and published in the parent's state (`temperature` of `camera:capabilities` and `focuser:updated`); its thermometer proxies are not a managed type. A camera reports a `cooler` only when it has a cooler switch or a settable target temperature, so a camera with a bare thermometer shows only its temperature.
- The mechanical type of a mount (`mountType`) comes from `TELESCOPE_MOUNT_TYPE` or the Alpaca `alignmentmode`, and is `UNKNOWN` until reported; only `ALTAZ` is evidence against a polar alignment, and the INDI base class reports `EQ_GEM` unless its driver says otherwise.
- Known driver differences: Alpaca mounts expose only Go Home (no find or set home, no set park, no flip); Alpaca domes cannot unpark or rotate manually; Alpaca focusers have no sync or reverse; Alpaca rotators have no home. Pier side and the INDI Alignment Subsystem are reported but have no commander, so the controls show them read-only; power, weather, safety monitor, and GPS devices are not managed. The vectors of all of them can still be seen and written in INDI Properties (see [INDI Properties Contract](indi-properties.md)). The simulator has no composite cover with a heater; that path is tested with a fake client.

# Star Detection Contract

Part of the [architecture](../ARCHITECTURE.md) of Nebulosa.

- One service, `StarDetectionService` (`api/src/stars.ts`, analysis jobs of the Shared Image Contracts with routes `/api/images/:id/stars` and `/api/stars`, event `stars:updated`), detects the stars of an image document for Images → Star Detection (manual runs) and Auto Focus. Captures never analyze their frames; Guiding, the stacking analyses, and the Star Profile Inspector run the toolkit's detector on their own. Results are the toolkit's `DetectedStar` (x, y, HFD, SNR, flux) in 0-based pixel coordinates of the document, origin at the upper-left sample, +X right, +Y down.
- Providers: `builtin` runs `detectStars`, the toolkit's port of ASTAP's star extraction (it finds the stars `astap` finds on the same image for the same minimum SNR, and measures each with the toolkit's centroid, HFD, and shape), on a worker of its own (`api/src/stars.worker.ts`), fed the grayscale copy of `ImageStore.detectionImage` (a CFA mosaic debayered with its trusted pattern first), one at a time, cancelled or stopped after 120 s (`timeout`) by terminating its worker. `astap` runs `astapDetectStars` on a FITS copy in a per-job temporary directory, one process at a time, killed through its signal, with the External Tools settings resolved once per job (or per Auto Focus session); a submission while ASTAP is not configured is `422 astapUnavailable`, and its failures are `astapUnavailable`, `astapTimeout`, or `astapFailed`. A failure never falls back to the other provider. An equivalent request is the same image, detector settings, acceptance, and ASTAP executable; only its kept result is reused, never a job still waiting or running.
- Accepted stars have finite coordinates inside the image less an edge margin, a finite positive HFD within the limits, and finite positive SNR (at least the minimum) and flux. The aggregate HFD is the median HFD of the accepted stars, in pixels, and is unavailable (never 0) below 3 accepted stars. HFDs of the two providers are computed differently and must not be compared.
- Image quality (`web/src/stars/quality.ts`, `ImageQualitySummary`) is derived from the latest star detection known for an image id, whoever asked for it, so the HFD an Auto Focus measured on a frame is shown with that frame without detecting again. Its states are `not-measured` (no detection, or the latest was cancelled or skipped), `pending`, `measured` (an HFD only when enough stars were accepted), and `failed`; absence is never zero, and the summary keeps the provider and request an HFD is only comparable under. The Image Inspector shows it in its Quality section; nothing is measured automatically, and per-frame quality history is not kept yet.
- The request (detector, its settings, acceptance) is one profile preference, `ProfileSettings.starDetection` (`starDetectionSettings`, `setStarDetectionSettings` in `web/src/profile/profile.ts`), edited in Images → Star Detection (also before the document has an image, as a camera tab waiting for its first frame, with Run Detection disabled until it has one) and shared by every document and by the frames of Auto Focus and Guiding; a document keeps only how its markers are shown. The built-in detector's "Aperture radius" (`maximumRadius`) defaults to 32 px (`DEFAULT_APERTURE_RADIUS` of `web/src/stars/stars.ts`), and the web client always sends it; the aperture grows only while a star extends beyond it, so focused stars measure as with the toolkit's fixed 4 px one, and only a star larger than ASTAP's 14 px search box is searched again in a box of that radius.
- Auto Focus integration: `StarDetectionService.measure(imageId, request, signal, origin)` resolves to the result of that image, whatever newer frame arrives, and cancels with its signal; it also takes a preparation resolved once, so a session keeps its ASTAP settings. Auto Focus jobs carry the `autofocus` origin.
- Limitations: a request without `maximumRadius` measures the built-in HFD inside the fixed 4 px aperture, which saturates near 8 px for defocused stars; the built-in detector returns at most 2000 stars (`BUILTIN_STAR_LIMIT`, the toolkit's `STAR_DETECTION_LIMIT`) and, as ASTAP, starts no star within 16 px of a frame edge (more for a star larger than its box: `starDetectionBorder`) and leaves out a faint star whose search box holds a much brighter neighbor, a trail longer than about 14 px, and a star under a streak; saturation is not detected; a frame replaced on screen before its result is fetched shows no markers.
